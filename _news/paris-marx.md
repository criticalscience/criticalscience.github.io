We are hosting Paris Marx on Friday, October 23rd!

We are thrilled to host [Paris Marx](https://parismarx.com/), journalist and tech critic internationally known for his podcast [Tech Won’t Save Us](https://techwontsave.us/). Paris is currently on tour presenting his new book [Hyperscale: The ambition and excess of big tech’s data empires](https://hyperscalebook.com/), and we’ve been lucky to catch him in Montreal and convince him to come talk to us at the Critical Science RG!
